Meet Anna

Origin

Half in a forest

I grew up half in a forest and half in a house where the emotional weather was unpredictable. Outside was safer than inside, so I spent my early years in nature, singing to everything and talking with whatever lived there.

At six I lost the forest and moved to a city, and with it went my sense of belonging to something larger. I spent the next twenty years chasing love and approval, in a career built on applause: in the music scene, as a radio speaker and then a talent agent, between London, Ibiza and Berlin.

In 2017 it all fell apart in a single week.

Everything I have done since has been the long way home. Yoga, meditation and my own voice became the path back: to my body, to the earth, and to the feeling of belonging. Flawsome Wellness was born on that road.

The core of my being
To live heart-based, embody the frequency of love, and guide others to do the same.
Calling

I lived the disconnection

From the earth, from our bodies, from each other, from our own purpose.

And underneath it, a fear that runs much of modern life: the fear of failure, tied almost entirely to money, which keeps good people small, isolated and stuck in systems that no longer serve them.

Systems built on fear are coming apart. The tragedy would be arriving at that moment without remembering who we are.

Why Flawsome?

You are Flawsome, just as you are

To me, Flawsome means having the courage to embrace and celebrate my unique qualities, imperfections included, with compassion and kindness.

It's about deeply loving myself as I am today, with all my strengths, skills, talents and quirks, and I want the people I work with to be able to do the same. Perfection is limiting. When we learn to love ourselves deeply, we can truly accept ourselves, and emotional authenticity becomes the foundation for unconditional self-love.

When you live in alignment with who you truly are, you naturally vibrate at a higher frequency. Health, happiness, abundance and joy become easier to create.

My quest

From fear-based living to love-based living

I help people and communities make that shift. It begins within: we harmonise our own vibration first, and a new mindset follows.

I want to preserve what is most human in us: the heart-based way of living; our connection to the earth, starting with our own earth body; and the wisdom of the ancestors. These are the three dimensions of regeneration: Self, Soil and Soul.

I work with people who have shrunk to stay safe, who have performed their way through life and never felt it land, who have lost touch with their own Self and voice. And with the creators of the new world, here to give birth to new paradigms rooted in love.

Today I steward Liminal Village, an eco-tech regenerative hub in Italy, and collaborate with NAO on care economies and trust-based systems.
