My story

A heart-based life, lived out loud

I'm an Italian-Swiss HEARTist and embodiment and voice coach. My work lives where voice alchemy, somatic intelligence, Bhakti Yoga and regenerative culture design meet.

I founded Flawsome Wellness more than nine years ago to bridge the SOMA, the heart-womb and authentic expression. I activate the voice as a portal for clarity, resonance and impact.

Through immersive experiences, retreats and mentorship, I guide people into deeper embodiment, confidence and creative expression.

The core of my being
To live heart-based, embody the frequency of love, and guide others to do the same.
Beyond the personal

Weaving new culture

I support visionaries and system creators in reclaiming their voice, aligning with purpose, and embodying coherence between body, heart and leadership.

My work extends into cultural regeneration: supporting and creating new systems, communities and relational ways of living rooted in care, trust and coherence. Today I steward Liminal Village, an eco-tech regenerative hub in Italy, and collaborate with NAO on care economies and trust-based systems.
