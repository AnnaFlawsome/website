Signature retreat · Liminal Village, Italy

Mama Italy: Ritualise your life and thrive

A 7-day immersive cultural retreat rooted in joy, devotion, and the art of coming home to yourself.

Hosted by a family from the Marche region, founders of a regenerative co-living hub, you explore the ecological community, practise rituals, eat and pick food from the land, and harmonise with the rhythms of Liminal Village, not as observers but as guests of a living organism.

Guided by rituals of initiation and devotion, ancient knowledge and cutting-edge tech, Bhakti, and modern subconscious and somatic practices, you remember how to rest, how to feel, how to belong, and how to return to your most natural frequency.

This is not about fixing. It's about feeling, and remembering.
