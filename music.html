The music

Songs as prayer

Anna Amrita is my collaboration with Ysmail Bhakti: mantra, devotional song and original compositions.

Rooted in Bhakti Yoga, the music is an invitation to sing, to feel, and to remember the love at the centre of things.

Ysmail is an artist, music therapist and voice embodiment coach, and a bridge between East and West. A multi-instrumentalist and composer, his journey began with a life-changing pilgrimage to Varanasi, India, followed by nine years of dedicated practice in Indian classical music. In Italy he specialised in music therapy for rehabilitation and social integration.
