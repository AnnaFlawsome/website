Short bio

Anna Giunta is an Italian-Swiss HEARTist and embodiment and voice coach working at the intersection of voice alchemy, somatic intelligence, Bhakti Yoga and regenerative culture design. She supports visionaries and system creators in reclaiming their voice, aligning with purpose, and embodying coherence between body, heart and leadership.

Long bio

Founder of Flawsome Wellness with more than nine years of experience, Anna bridges the SOMA, the heart-womb and authentic expression, activating voice as a portal for clarity, resonance and impact. Through immersive experiences, retreats and mentorship, she guides individuals into deeper embodiment, confidence and creative expression.

Beyond personal transformation, Anna's work extends into cultural regeneration: supporting and creating new systems, communities and relational ways of living rooted in care, trust and coherence. The core of her being is to live heart-based, embody the frequency of love, and guide others to do the same.
