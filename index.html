Welcome, beautiful soul

The long way home

I grew up half in a forest, singing to everything that lived there. Then came the city, and twenty years of chasing love and approval in a career built on applause. In 2017 it all fell apart in a single week.

Everything I've done since has been the long way home. Today I help people and communities move from fear-based living to love-based living, through voice, body and community.

My mission

Harmonise the Self. Root in the Soil. Remember the Soul.

From fear-based living to love-based living, in three dimensions of regeneration, because change that stays in only one of them doesn't last.

Self

The inner world: your body, nervous system and voice.

Soil

The local: the place and the people around you, where a community puts down roots.

Soul

The world: the larger whole we belong to, and the shift from fear-based systems to love-based ones.

Voice, body and community turned out to be one river, running through all three.
